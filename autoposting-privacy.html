Privacy Policy — Autoposting

This is an internal marketing automation tool used by Faisal Khan to schedule and publish posts to LinkedIn Company Pages and Instagram Business Accounts that we own and administer.

What data we collect

We do not collect data from end users — there are no end users. The application processes only:

  • Post captions and images authored by our marketing team
  • LinkedIn access tokens for our own Company Pages
  • Post URNs returned by LinkedIn after we publish our own posts

How we store data

Access tokens are stored only on our private infrastructure with restricted file permissions. No data is shared with third parties.
